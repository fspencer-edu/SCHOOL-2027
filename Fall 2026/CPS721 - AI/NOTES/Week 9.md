# Historical Notes

- GPS (General Problem Solver)
	- Developed by Allen Newell and Herbert Simon in the 1960s
		- Performs "means-ends analysis"
		- Tries to pick actions to make current state more similar to goal
		- Inefficient, but started things off with a very ambitious project
- Modern day planning and often done in single language PDDL
	- General systems that take in any problem written in PDDL and try to solve them
