## The Laws of Thought Approach

-  Syllogism
	- Aristotle
	- Irrefutable reasoning processes
	- Patterns for argument structures that always yields a correct conclusions given the correct premises
- Logicians
	- Notation for statements about objects in the worlds and relations
- Logicist
	- Build on programs to create intelligent systems
- Probability
	- Rigorous reasoning with uncertain information
	- Comprehensive model of rational thought from raw perceptual information
	- Does not generate intelligent behaviour

## The Rational Agent Approach

- Agent
	- Something that acts
	- Operate autonomously, perceive their environment, persist over a prolonged time period, adapt to change, and create and pursue goals
- Rational agent
	- Acts to achieve the best outcome when there is uncertainty
	- Deduce that a given action is best and then act on that conclusion
	- More general that the "laws of thought"
		- Correct inference is one of the several possible mechanisms for rationality
	- Amenable to scientific development
- Control theory/standard model
	- A controller minimizes a cost function
	- Policy maximizes a sum of rewards
	- Rule minimizes a loss function
	- Decision maker maximizes utility of some measure
- Limited rationality
	- Acting when there is not enough time to do all the computations
