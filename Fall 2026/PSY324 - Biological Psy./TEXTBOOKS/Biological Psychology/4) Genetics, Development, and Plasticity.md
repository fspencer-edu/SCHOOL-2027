### Competition Among Axons

- When axon reach their targets, chemical gradients steer them to their current location
- Not perfect accuracy
- If a neuron cannot find the target, it forms synapses oto another target
- Each postsynaptic cell strengthens the most appropriate synapses and eliminates others
- Neural Darwinism
	- In the development of the nervous system
	- Start with more neurons and synapses than we can keep
	- Selection process keeps the most successful combinations of synapses and rejects others
- Neurotrophins steer new axonal branches and synapses in approximately the right direction
