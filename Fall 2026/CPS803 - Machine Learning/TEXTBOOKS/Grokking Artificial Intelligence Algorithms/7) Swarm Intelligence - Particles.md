# What is particle swarm optimization?

- Particle swarm optimization
	- Another swarm algorithm
- Alignment
	- An individual should steer in the average heading of its neighbours
- Cohesion
	- An individual should move toward the average position of its neighbours
- Separation
	- An individual should avoid crowding or colliding with its neighbours

- Involves a group of individuals at different points in the solution space, using swarm concepts to find an optimal solution

# Optimization problems: A slightly more technical perspective

- Particle swarm optimization algorithms are good at solving difficult optimization problems
- Distributed over the multi-dimensional search space and work together to find good max or mins
- Large search spaces
- Search spaces with high dimensions
# Problems applicable to particle swarm optimization

- Particle swarm optimization provides a means to search a large search space without checking every value in each dimension
# Representing state: What do particles look like?

## Particle swarm optimization life cycle

- Each problem has a unique context and a different domain in which data is represented
- Solutions to different problems are also measured differently
- Initialize the population of particles
- Calculate the fitness of each particle
- Update the position of each particle
- Determine the stopping criteria

### Initializing population

- Number of particles
- Starting position for each particle
- Starting velocity for each particle

### Calculating fitness

- Fitness of particles is calculating every time the entire swarm changes position

### Update position

- Particles in the swarm update their position given a cognitive ability and factors in the environment
