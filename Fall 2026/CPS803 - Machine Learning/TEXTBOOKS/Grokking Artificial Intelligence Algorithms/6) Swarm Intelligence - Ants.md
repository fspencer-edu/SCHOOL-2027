# What is swarm intelligence?

- Swarm intelligence
	- A subset of evolutionary algorithms
	- Nature inspired algorithms
	- Useful for solving optimization problems where several constraints need to be bet in a specific problem space and an absolute best solution is difficult to find in may possible solutions
- Clustering
- Optimization
- Routing
- Scheduling

- Ant colony optimization (ACO)
